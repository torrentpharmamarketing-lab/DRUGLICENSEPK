Why Most Applications Fail

These 4 Mistakes Are Costing Applicants
3–6 Months of Delays

You are not the only one confused. These are the most common — and most expensive — mistakes people make when applying for a Drug Sale License in Pakistan.

❌
Going to DRAP before registering with SECP and FBR DRAP will not process your application without your SECP certificate and NTN. Thousands of applicants discover this after weeks of waiting — then have to start over.
❌
Not attaching a Pakistan Pharmacy Council pharmacist first DRAP requires a registered pharmacist named on your application before it is accepted. This step surprises almost every first-time applicant and causes instant rejection.
❌
Uploading black & white scans or unclear photos The portal requires colour scans only. Blurry uploads, medicines visible in shop photos, or missing signatures on Form 8A all trigger an Aitraaz (objection) notice.
❌
Selecting the wrong license category Pharma franchise businesses need a Wholesale Drug Sale License — not a retail pharmacy license. Selecting the wrong category is one of the top rejection reasons.
